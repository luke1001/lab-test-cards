Chemistry

TCH

Total Cholesterol

Pronounced: toh-tuhl koh-less-ter-awl

Open SVG

What it is

A chemistry test measuring the total cholesterol amount in blood.

Why it's ordered

  • Screen cardiovascular risk markers
  • Part of lipid testing
  • Monitor cholesterol trends
  • May be ordered alone or in a panel

Common specimen

Serum or plasma. Common tube/container: Gold/SST, mint green PST, or local chemistry tube; verify local policy.

Lab helper cue

  • Route to chemistry
  • Check if fasting instructions apply locally
  • Separate serum or plasma as required

Memory hook

TCH is the total cholesterol number.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
