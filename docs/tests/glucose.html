What it is

A chemistry test measuring blood sugar at the collection time.

Why it's ordered

  • Screen high or low glucose
  • Monitor diabetes care
  • Evaluate metabolic symptoms
  • Common emergency test

Common specimen

Serum or plasma. Common tube/container: Gray, gold/SST, or green tube depending on site.

Lab helper cue

  • Tube choice varies by workflow
  • Process promptly when required
  • Route to chemistry or point-of-care area

Memory hook
