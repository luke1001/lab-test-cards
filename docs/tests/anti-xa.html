Anti-Factor Xa Activity

Pronounced: an-tee factor ten-ay

Open SVG

What it is

A coagulation test that measures heparin-related anti-factor Xa activity.

Why it's ordered

  • Monitor heparin therapy
  • Check low-molecular-weight heparin effect
  • Support anticoagulation dosing workflows
  • Often time-sensitive after dosing

Common specimen

Citrated plasma. Common tube/container: Light blue sodium citrate tube; verify local timing and handling policy.

Lab helper cue

  • Confirm collection time
  • Fill blue tube to mark
  • Route promptly to coag

Memory hook

Anti-Xa is the blue-tube heparin activity check.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
