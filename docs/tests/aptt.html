Coagulation

aPTT

Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time

Pronounced: ay-pee-tee-tee

Open SVG

What it is

A coagulation test that evaluates another part of the clotting pathway.

Why it's ordered

  • Monitor unfractionated heparin
  • Screen clotting pathway issues
  • Investigate bleeding concerns
  • Often paired with PT/INR

Common specimen

Citrated plasma. Common tube/container: Light blue sodium citrate tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Correct fill is critical
  • Avoid clots
  • Route quickly if STAT

Memory hook

aPTT is the heparin-watch clot timer.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
