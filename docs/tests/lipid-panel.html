Lipid Panel

Pronounced: lip-id panel

Open SVG

What it is

A chemistry panel measuring cholesterol and triglyceride-related values.

Why it's ordered

  • Assess cardiovascular risk markers
  • Monitor lipid therapy
  • Screen cholesterol patterns
  • May have fasting rules by site

Common specimen

Serum or plasma. Common tube/container: Gold/SST, mint green PST, or local chemistry tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Ask about fasting only if local policy requires
  • Route to chemistry
  • Separate sample as required

Memory hook

Lipids are the blood fat family.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
