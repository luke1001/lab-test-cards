Albumin

Pronounced: al-byoo-min

Open SVG

What it is

A chemistry blood test that measures the main protein made by the liver.

Why it's ordered

  • Assess liver-related protein status
  • Check nutrition and illness trends
  • Evaluate kidney or fluid-loss concerns
  • Included in many chemistry panels

Common specimen

Serum or plasma. Common tube/container: Gold SST, red, or green tube; verify local policy.

Lab helper cue

  • Often part of CMP or hepatic panel
  • Route to chemistry
  • Avoid hemolyzed or short samples

Memory hook

Albumin is the chemistry protein that often rides with liver panels.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
