Urinalysis

Pronounced: you-ay

Open SVG

What it is

A urine test that checks physical, chemical, and microscopic findings.

Why it's ordered

  • Screen urinary tract concerns
  • Check kidney or metabolic clues
  • Evaluate pregnancy or pre-op workflows when paired with other tests
  • Common ED and outpatient test

Common specimen

Urine. Common tube/container: Clean urine cup or preservative tube per site.

Lab helper cue

  • Label and cap container well
  • Process promptly or preserve
  • Microscopy rules vary by site

Memory hook

UA reads the urine story from strip to scope.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
