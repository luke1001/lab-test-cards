Amylase

Pronounced: am-ih-lays

Open SVG

What it is

A chemistry enzyme test commonly associated with pancreatic and digestive workups.

Why it's ordered

  • Evaluate pancreatic enzyme activity
  • Support abdominal pain workups
  • May be paired with lipase
  • Sometimes ordered on urine or body fluid

Common specimen

Serum, plasma, urine, or body fluid depending on order. Common tube/container: Gold SST, red, green, or approved fluid container; verify local policy.

Lab helper cue

  • Check specimen source
  • Route to chemistry
  • Use correct container for fluids

Memory hook

Amylase is the enzyme card: source matters before routing.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
