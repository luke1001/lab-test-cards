Coagulation

PT/INR

Prothrombin Time / International Normalized Ratio

Pronounced: pro-throm-bin time, eye-en-are

Open SVG

What it is

A coagulation test that evaluates part of the clotting pathway.

Why it's ordered

  • Monitor warfarin therapy
  • Screen clotting factor issues
  • Check bleeding risk before procedures
  • Often ordered with aPTT

Common specimen

Citrated plasma. Common tube/container: Light blue sodium citrate tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Fill blue tube to mark
  • Mix gently
  • Check clots and proper ratio

Memory hook

PT/INR needs the blue tube filled right.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
