Lactic Acid

Pronounced: lack-tate

Open SVG

What it is

A time-sensitive chemistry test associated with tissue oxygen and illness stress patterns.

Why it's ordered

  • Evaluate possible sepsis or shock
  • Monitor critical illness trends
  • Assess poor perfusion concerns
  • Often handled as urgent

Common specimen

Plasma or whole blood. Common tube/container: Gray or green tube, site-specific.

Lab helper cue

  • STAT handling is common
  • Transport quickly per policy
  • Ice rules vary by analyzer and site

Memory hook

Lactate likes speed: collect, route, process.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
