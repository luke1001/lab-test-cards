Hematology

Retic

Reticulocyte Count

Pronounced: reh-tik-yoo-lo-site count

Open SVG

What it is

A count of young red blood cells recently released from marrow.

Why it's ordered

  • Evaluate marrow response
  • Work up anemia patterns
  • Monitor recovery after blood loss
  • Often reviewed with CBC results

Common specimen

Whole blood. Common tube/container: Lavender EDTA tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Route to hematology
  • Avoid clotted samples
  • May require fresh specimen timing

Memory hook

Retic means young RBCs reporting for duty.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
