Procal

Procalcitonin

Pronounced: pro-kal-sih-toh-nin

Open SVG

What it is

A chemistry blood test used as an infection and sepsis marker.

Why it's ordered

  • Help assess serious infection
  • Support sepsis workups
  • Trend response in hospitalized patients
  • Often ordered with cultures and lactate

Common specimen

Serum or plasma. Common tube/container: Gold SST or green lithium heparin tube; verify local policy.

Lab helper cue

  • Check STAT priority
  • Route to chemistry promptly
  • Follow local tube rules

Memory hook

Procal often travels with sepsis labs.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
