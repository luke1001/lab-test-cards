SPIFE

Serum Protein Immunofixation Electrophoresis

Pronounced: ess-pie-fee

Open SVG

What it is

A serum test that identifies specific immunoglobulin protein bands.

Why it's ordered

  • Follow up abnormal serum protein patterns
  • Look for monoclonal protein bands
  • Evaluate plasma cell disorder workups
  • Often paired with SPEP or free light chains

Common specimen

Serum. Common tube/container: Gold/SST or red-top serum tube; verify local policy.

Lab helper cue

  • Route to chemistry or sendout area
  • Allow serum to clot and separate as required
  • Check local stability and minimum volume

Memory hook

SPIFE fixes the protein band identity after a serum protein pattern is seen.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
