HEPFA

Hepatic Function Panel

Pronounced: heh-pat-ik funk-shun panel

Open SVG

What it is

A chemistry panel that groups common liver-related enzymes, bilirubin, and protein markers.

Why it's ordered

  • Screen liver-related marker patterns
  • Monitor known liver or bile duct concerns
  • Review medication or illness effects
  • Often ordered with BMP or CMP

Common specimen

Serum or plasma. Common tube/container: Gold/SST, mint green PST, or local chemistry tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Route to chemistry
  • Separate serum or plasma as required
  • Hemolysis or icterus checks may matter by analyzer

Memory hook

HEPFA is the focused liver chemistry snapshot.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
