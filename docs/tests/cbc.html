CBC

Complete Blood Count

Pronounced: see-bee-see

Open SVG

What it is

A common hematology test that counts major blood cell groups.

Why it's ordered

  • Screen anemia or infection clues
  • Check platelets before procedures
  • Monitor blood loss or therapy
  • Often ordered with a differential

Common specimen

Whole blood. Common tube/container: Lavender EDTA tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Mix EDTA tubes gently
  • Route to hematology
  • Watch for clots or short draws

Memory hook

CBC counts the cells that carry, fight, and clot.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
