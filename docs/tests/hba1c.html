HbA1c

Hemoglobin A1c

Pronounced: hee-moh-gloh-bin ay-one-see

Open SVG

What it is

A test reflecting longer-term glucose exposure through hemoglobin glycation.

Why it's ordered

  • Monitor diabetes care
  • Screen glucose control patterns
  • Useful because it is not a single moment glucose
  • Often ordered with metabolic labs

Common specimen

Whole blood. Common tube/container: Lavender EDTA tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Route per local analyzer area
  • Mix EDTA tube gently
  • Usually not handled like serum chemistry

Memory hook

A1c is sugar history attached to hemoglobin.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
