Urine Culture

Pronounced: you-rin kul-chur

Open SVG

What it is

A microbiology test that checks urine for bacterial growth.

Why it's ordered

  • Evaluate possible urinary infection
  • Identify organism growth
  • Support susceptibility workflow if positive
  • Often paired with urinalysis

Common specimen

Urine. Common tube/container: Sterile cup or preservative urine tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Clean-catch quality matters
  • Preserve or refrigerate per policy
  • Route to microbiology

Memory hook

Culture asks: what grows from this urine?

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
