Phos

Phosphorus

Pronounced: foss-for-us

Open SVG

What it is

A chemistry test measuring phosphorus in blood.

Why it's ordered

  • Evaluate kidney-related mineral balance
  • Monitor nutrition or replacement
  • Review bone/mineral concerns
  • Often paired with calcium and magnesium

Common specimen

Serum or plasma. Common tube/container: Gold/SST, mint green PST, or local chemistry tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Route to chemistry
  • Separate per local timing
  • Confirm tube and stability for add-ons

Memory hook

Phos pairs with calcium in the mineral balance story.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
