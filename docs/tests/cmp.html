CMP

Comprehensive Metabolic Panel

Pronounced: see-em-pee

Open SVG

What it is

A broader chemistry panel that includes BMP-style tests plus liver proteins and enzymes.

Why it's ordered

  • Review kidney and electrolyte status
  • Screen liver-related markers
  • Check protein and albumin
  • Common wellness and inpatient panel

Common specimen

Serum or plasma. Common tube/container: Gold/SST, mint green PST, or local chemistry tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Route to chemistry
  • Separate serum/plasma on time
  • Verify local tube preference

Memory hook

CMP is BMP plus a liver-and-protein view.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
