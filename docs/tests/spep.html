SPEP

Serum Protein Electrophoresis

Pronounced: ess-pep

Open SVG

What it is

A serum test that separates blood proteins into visible electrophoresis patterns.

Why it's ordered

  • Screen for abnormal serum protein patterns
  • Evaluate possible monoclonal protein
  • Support plasma cell disorder workups
  • Often followed by immunofixation testing

Common specimen

Serum. Common tube/container: Gold/SST or red-top serum tube; verify local policy.

Lab helper cue

  • Route to chemistry or sendout area
  • Separate serum as required
  • Avoid plasma unless local procedure allows it

Memory hook

SPEP separates serum proteins into a pattern before band identity is confirmed.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
