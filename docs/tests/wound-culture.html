Wound Culture

Pronounced: woond kul-chur

Open SVG

What it is

A microbiology test that checks wound material for organism growth.

Why it's ordered

  • Evaluate infected wound concerns
  • Identify possible organisms
  • Support susceptibility workflow if indicated
  • Guide provider follow-up

Common specimen

Wound swab, tissue, or aspirate. Common tube/container: Culture swab transport or sterile container.

Lab helper cue

  • Specimen quality matters
  • Aspirate or tissue may be preferred
  • Route to microbiology

Memory hook

Better wound specimens give better culture answers.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
