Cardiac

BNP

B-type Natriuretic Peptide

Pronounced: bee-en-pee

Open SVG

What it is

A cardiac-related marker often used when fluid overload or heart failure is being evaluated.

Why it's ordered

  • Assess shortness of breath workups
  • Support heart failure evaluation
  • Monitor cardiac-related fluid status
  • Often ordered from ED or inpatient areas

Common specimen

Plasma or serum. Common tube/container: Lavender, green, or SST depending on platform.

Lab helper cue

  • Verify analyzer tube type
  • Route to chemistry or immunoassay area
  • Check local stability rules

Memory hook

BNP is the heart's fluid-stress signal.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
