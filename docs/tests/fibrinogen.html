Fibrinogen

Fibrinogen

Pronounced: fye-brin-oh-jen

Open SVG

What it is

A coagulation test measuring fibrinogen, a protein needed for clot formation.

Why it's ordered

  • Assess bleeding or clotting disorders
  • Evaluate DIC-related concerns
  • Monitor major bleeding workflows
  • Often ordered with other coag tests

Common specimen

Citrated plasma. Common tube/container: Light blue sodium citrate tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Correct fill matters
  • Avoid clotted specimens
  • Route to coagulation

Memory hook

Fibrinogen is the fiber-maker for clots.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
