ESR

Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate

Pronounced: ee-rith-ro-site sed-ih-men-tay-shun rate

Open SVG

What it is

A timed test that measures how quickly red cells settle.

Why it's ordered

  • Screen for inflammation patterns
  • Follow chronic inflammatory conditions
  • Often paired with CRP
  • Not a STAT-style test in many labs

Common specimen

Whole blood. Common tube/container: Lavender EDTA or site-specific ESR tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Timing and tube type vary
  • Keep tube well mixed
  • Verify local ESR method

Memory hook

ESR is red cells settling in a timed column.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
