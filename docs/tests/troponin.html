Troponin

Cardiac Troponin

Pronounced: troh-poh-nin

Open SVG

What it is

A cardiac marker commonly used in chest pain and heart injury evaluations.

Why it's ordered

  • Evaluate possible heart injury
  • Trend changes over time
  • Support emergency chest pain workflows
  • Often handled STAT

Common specimen

Serum or plasma. Common tube/container: Gold/SST or green tube depending on analyzer.

Lab helper cue

  • Route quickly for STAT orders
  • Tube type varies by platform
  • Repeat timing is ordered by provider

Memory hook

Troponin is the heart-injury marker to move fast.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
