Throat Culture

Pronounced: throte kul-chur

Open SVG

What it is

A microbiology test that checks throat swabs for specific organism growth.

Why it's ordered

  • Evaluate strep or throat infection concerns
  • Confirm rapid-test follow-up when ordered
  • Support organism identification
  • Common outpatient or ED test

Common specimen

Throat swab. Common tube/container: Culture swab transport.

Lab helper cue

  • Use correct swab transport
  • Label source clearly
  • Route to microbiology

Memory hook

Throat culture grows the answer from the swab.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
