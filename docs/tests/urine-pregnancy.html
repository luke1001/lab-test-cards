Urine Pregnancy Test

Pronounced: you-rin aych-see-gee

Open SVG

What it is

A urine test that detects hCG associated with pregnancy.

Why it's ordered

  • Screen pregnancy status
  • Pre-procedure or medication workflows
  • Evaluate missed period concerns
  • Often rapid or point-of-care

Common specimen

Urine. Common tube/container: Clean urine cup.

Lab helper cue

  • Route per local POCT or lab process
  • Avoid unlabeled cups
  • Check minimum volume

Memory hook

hCG is the pregnancy signal in urine.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
