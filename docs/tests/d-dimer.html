D-dimer

Pronounced: dee-dye-mer

Open SVG

What it is

A coagulation test related to clot breakdown products.

Why it's ordered

  • Evaluate clot-related concerns
  • Support emergency rule-out workflows
  • Assess DIC-related patterns
  • Often ordered urgently

Common specimen

Citrated plasma. Common tube/container: Light blue sodium citrate tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Fill blue tube correctly
  • STAT routing may apply
  • Check local rejection rules

Memory hook

D-dimer points to clot breakdown debris.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
