Blood Culture

Pronounced: blud kul-chur

Open SVG

What it is

A microbiology test that looks for organisms growing from blood culture bottles.

Why it's ordered

  • Evaluate bloodstream infection
  • Support fever or sepsis workups
  • Guide organism identification workflow
  • Usually collected before antibiotics when ordered

Common specimen

Blood. Common tube/container: Aerobic and anaerobic culture bottles.

Lab helper cue

  • Strict collection order and antisepsis matter
  • Do not refrigerate unless policy says
  • Route promptly to microbiology

Memory hook

Blood cultures need clean collection and fast incubation.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
