BMP

Basic Metabolic Panel

Pronounced: bee-em-pee

Open SVG

What it is

A chemistry panel for electrolytes, glucose, kidney markers, and calcium.

Why it's ordered

  • Check electrolyte balance
  • Assess kidney function markers
  • Monitor glucose
  • Common emergency and inpatient panel

Common specimen

Serum or plasma. Common tube/container: Gold/SST, mint green PST, or local chemistry tube.

Lab helper cue

  • Route to chemistry
  • Centrifuge serum/plasma as required
  • Watch hemolysis for potassium impact

Memory hook

BMP is the basic chemistry snapshot.

Verify specimen, tube, transport, and handling requirements against your local policy.
